# Dusseldorp Forum. The ask.
No form or due date. $50,000. Use undecided: 66 Stretch Beds at $750, or the Empathy Ledger media campaign Rachel asked about.

Dear Rachel,

On 6 September I promised you a concrete proposal. Here it is.

The Butterfly Movement Ltd, trading as Goods on Country, asks the Dusseldorp Forum for $50,000. There are two ways it could be used, and we would like your view on which fits the Forum better.

The first is beds. $50,000 buys 66 Stretch Beds at $750 each, delivered to families in remote Aboriginal communities who are sleeping on floors. The price covers the recycled-plastic legs pressed from 20 kg of diverted plastic, the washable canvas, steel poles and hardware, freight to the community, and the paid time of local people who assemble and deliver each bed. A community organisation holds the stock and decides who receives a bed, and if it sells beds the full $750 stays with it.

The second is the Indigenous media campaign you asked about, run through Empathy Ledger. Empathy Ledger is the consent system that records what each storyteller has agreed may be published and where. $50,000 would fund the campaign's production and the paid storytellers, with every story published only on recorded consent and with the storyteller able to withdraw it.

Either way, nothing in this ask is for our running costs.

540 beds are already in homes across eleven communities, each recorded against a batch, a community and a date. 320 of those were bought and paid for by four organisations. The Forum's earlier $15,000 for CONTAINED helped get us here.

You would receive the asset register extract for any beds funded, or the published campaign with its consent records. We do not claim a health outcome from a bed. We count a person off the floor, a paid hour of work and a kilogram of plastic.

Ben Knight
Goods on Country
