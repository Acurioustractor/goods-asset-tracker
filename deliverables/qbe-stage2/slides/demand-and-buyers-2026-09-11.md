# Slides: the buyers, and the demand

Copy settled 11 September 2026. Every figure sits in `v2/src/lib/data/demand-and-buyers.ts`
with 26 guards. Nothing here is printed from a spreadsheet.

## Slide A: what people have already paid for

**Four organisations have paid us for 320 beds.**
Centrecorp Foundation, Mala'la Health Service, Homeland School Company and the ALIVE National
Centre. Five invoices, every one settled. They paid $273,966, which is $247,770 of trade once
GST comes out.

**The price a buyer pays has gone from $370 to $800.**
Centrecorp paid $370 a bed for the first sixty in August 2025. ALIVE paid $800 each for a
hundred in July. Our list price is $750.

**They paid $50,000 for the making as well as the beds.**
Ten community-build and program-support visits, billed between $3,000 and $8,000 each and
settled with the bed lines on the same invoices.

**The forty Maningrida beds were pressed at our plant and put together at Gamardi**
with young people and Homeland School Company. Homeland paid $750 a bed, $5,900 of freight and
$8,000 for the trip.

Sources: Xero, Nicholas Marchesi sole trader, ABN 21 591 780 066 · invoices INV-0259, INV-0283,
INV-0291, INV-0303, INV-0342

## Slide B: what is wanted, and what is owned

**778 more beds are written down as wanted, across five communities.**
Every one has a date and a person or an organisation against it. None of them is an order.

**Twenty have a person and money behind them.**
Dianne Stokes offered to self-fund twenty at Tennant Creek. She is the Warumungu and Warlmanpa Elder who named
the washing machines Pakkimjalki Kari.

**A hundred and fifty at Utopia have a rule and no name.**
Beds for every child. It is the clearest denominator a community has given us and nobody has
been asked to pay for it.

**The other 758 are conversations.**
Five hundred of them are one meeting at Groote, still logged as exploring, and that record is
64% of the total on its own.

**Turning a conversation into an order is a trip to a community.**
Buyers have already paid us $50,000 to do that work.

Sources: `community_demand`, Goods v2 project, read 11 September 2026 · six records, largest
first
