# Three real forms arrived, and each changed its application

12 September 2026. Ben supplied the QBE final questions page, the Tim Fairfax form and the SEFA
Backing the Bold EOI. All three Notion pages are updated against the real wording.

## The finding that outranks everything else

**Jay's steer, recorded on the QBE final application page:**

> Capital, whether that's debt or equity, will be more favourably looked at rather than
> philanthropy. In theory, they don't want to use their corporate philanthropy to unlock other
> corporate philanthropy... this year they really want to prove this concept that corporate
> philanthropy can be used to unlock impact investment... if the whole thing is philanthropy, that
> will be something the Steerco will have to discuss, because that's a big change this year.

**Our $600,000 raise is 100% philanthropy.** QBE, Tim Fairfax, Brian M. Davis and Snow are all
grants. There is no debt and no equity in it, which is precisely the shape Jay warned against.

Jay also gave the benchmark: last year one enterprise asked for $400,000, unlocked $1.1M and
received $350,000. Twelve organisations are invited this year.

**A11, the Backing the Bold EOI, already exists as a draft and is unsent.** It is the fix, and the
Queensland stream is open with rolling acceptance. A letter of intent does not have to be a signed
deal. Sending it is worth more to the QBE application than any wording change anywhere.

## QBE

The real form is on the page. Q1 and Q2 allow **20,000 characters each**, so length constrains
nothing. Seven upload fields, five files each, 10MB a file.

A full attachment register is now on the page: three artifacts ready, seven needing work, three that
do not exist. **The accountant's letter is recorded on that page as the submission blocker and it
has no owner beside it.**

Q2 asks five things in one field and should be read against our answer once: which entity applies,
how related entities connect, how funds flow, why the grant sits there, and how capital raised
elsewhere supports the enterprise.

## Tim Fairfax

It is **FY27 Resilience: Multi-Year General Operating Support**, and it is a different application
from the one I built. Thirteen pages, and it tests governance maturity more than it tests the model.

**Fourteen required attachments. We hold three.** Missing: a strategic plan with KPIs current for
two years, the constitution, board sub-committees, a board skills matrix, a risk framework, a letter
of support from a beneficiary, a peer referee and an annual report.

Two of those are nearly free, because **TFFF supplies templates** for the skills matrix and the risk
framework.

The three-year case is still the argument and it belongs in question 3.7, which has a 200-word
guide. Everything else is new work.

**The form requires an AI use declaration.** TFFF asks organisations to declare any use of AI tools
in preparing the application and says plainly that it will not reject an application because AI was
used. The honest ticks are drafting answers later edited by staff, and preparing or reviewing
uploaded documents.

**The constitution now blocks two funders.** QBE Q12 and Q22, and TFFF 4.1, which is a required
field.

## SEFA Backing the Bold

The Queensland stream is **open, rolling, no closing date**. The EOI takes under fifteen minutes.

**One required field decides the application: what percentage of revenue comes from trade, plus
EBITDA for FY26, FY25, FY24 and FY23.**

| | Butterfly | The trading entity |
|---|---|---|
| FY26 income | $77,080 | about $713,827 |
| FY26 result | expenses $119,934, so EBITDA about negative $42,854 | roughly $168,000 before founder wages |
| Revenue from trade | almost none | all of it |
| FY23 to FY25 | a dormant shell | where the history is |

Applying as Butterfly makes the loan look unfundable. Applying as the trading entity makes it
fundable and raises the question of which entity repays. **Joel flagged exactly this on 21 August
and offered a call with Tanya from the investment team to look at the financials.** That call should
happen before the EOI goes.

A sensible first ask is $50,000 to $100,000: the second press at $22,500 plus working capital at
$276 a bed. At $474 a bed, 101 paid beds a year services $200,000.
