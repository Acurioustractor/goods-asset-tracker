How to read the voices

Every quote on this page is external tier in the repo storyteller registry, which is the record that governs what may be printed. The registry wins over Empathy Ledger, where nothing is yet approved for public display. Kristy Bloomfield is cleared as a person and is deliberately not quoted here: her quotes trace to other projects, so using them for Goods would be a scope error.

Answers are the text in deliverables/qbe-stage2/qbe-answers-2026-09-10.md and its Q9 attachment, rendered verbatim. Figures come from a recalculated copy of the workbook, because the file is saved without cached values.
