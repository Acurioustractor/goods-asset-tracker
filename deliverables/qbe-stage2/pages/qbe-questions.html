The year's figure was rebuilt on 11 September. The earlier $937,550 added 400 beds at the $750 sale price to the full running cost, and a bed at $750 already pays its own making and hands $474 to the organisation. The overlap was $189,600.

The gap is not in the plants. Plant money arrives in $150,000 pieces that match the $150,000 cost, so a plant either happens or it does not. The gap is 187 beds of first stock and a year of running the organisation. The $150,000 approved for Alice Springs and a likely second $150,000 add plants without moving the gap, unless the second one lands on a site QBE is already being asked to fund.

How much of the impact measure is finished

One of four measures is verified. A person off the floor is counted from the asset register, bed by bed, with a community and a date against each.

One has no enterprise behind it yet. Money kept in community is a settled rule, the full $750, and there is no community trading entity yet earning against it. Until one trades, it is a promise with a price on it.
