01Where it stands

The applicant is The Butterfly Movement Ltd trading as Goods on Country. It asks QBE for $300,000 to build two community plants, working choices Palm Island and Maningrida, with one plant at $150,000 as the smaller case. Beds are a separate ask and are not in this request.

Three dates sit the wrong way round

QBE wants any pre-conditions met by 13 November. The Brian M. Davis board sits on 19 November and Tim Fairfax decides in late November. So the two funders who pay for beds decide after QBE needs its conditions satisfied. Q18 puts that question to QBE directly.

02The model in four answers

Four questions carry the whole thing. Every figure below is a cell in the workbook.

What is a bed worth

$750 at the factory door. $276 to make it, with $80 of paid making inside that. $474 stays with Goods on Country. Freight is charged on top at cost, its own line per community, paid by whoever buys the bed. This is a price model. The organisation is never divided by beds alone.

What is being asked for

QBE is asked for $300,000 to build two plants at $150,000 each. Beds are separate: 400 of first stock, of which Tim Fairfax covers 133 and Brian M. Davis 80, leaving 187 to find, or 54 once the $100,000 Snow ask is sent and counted as beds. Alice Springs is funded through Oonchiumpa and sits outside the raise.

What already runs

320 beds sold and paid across ten invoices, $343,481 all time. FY26 Goods receipts were $653,246. Snow was $375,000 of that, or 57%, and there is no Snow ask this year. Nothing in the plan replaces the other $275,000.

What one bed does

Two hours of paid making. 36 kg of shred through the press, 20 kg of it in the finished bed. Up to $750 kept in the community when the community sells it. One person off the floor. Health is the reason the hardware exists and is never claimed as an outcome.

04Every claim

Every number and assertion that could reach a funder, with where it comes from and how far it can be defended. Five grades. Verified means the source was read directly. Modelled means it is calculated and has not been measured. Invited means a funder asked us to apply and has not decided. Conflict means two sources disagree and a person has to rule. Unverified means one source and no second.
