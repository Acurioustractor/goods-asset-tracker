# Flat-pack route correction, 12 September 2026

The workbook and source model bought legs, locally pressed tabs and community assembly. One press supplies 6 kits/day, 120/month at full availability and 96/month at 80% availability. A 400-kit run takes 4.1667 operating months. A second press is constrained by CNC at 136 whole kits/month.

Unknown purchased-panel yield and shred price block the calculator cost total. The financial cost basis is marked provisional. Historical invoices and funding receipts are preserved. A separate Community assembly tab records actuals.

Regression fixtures verified that abundant shred with zero legs produces zero kits, and that adding cut legs restores the corresponding kit quantity. Core model guards passed before the live correction.
