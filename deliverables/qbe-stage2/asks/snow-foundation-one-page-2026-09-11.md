## Goods on Country: 133 beds of first trading stock

**The ask.** $100,000, as a grant, to The Butterfly Movement Ltd (ABN 22 155 132 684), trading as
Goods on Country. Subject to your board.

**What it buys.** 133 Stretch Beds at $750 each, which become the first trading stock of a community
enterprise. The enterprise sells them to buyers who already hold health and housing budgets, and
keeps the money. Nothing comes back to Goods.

**Why beds.** A Stretch Bed is washable, sits off the ground and is made from 20 kg of recycled
plastic. Households in remote communities are sleeping on floors, and the reason that matters is
scabies and the path it runs to rheumatic heart disease. We do not claim a health outcome. We claim
a person off the floor, and we count it bed by bed against a register.

**What is already true.** 540 beds are deployed across eleven communities. 320 beds have been bought
and paid for by four organisations for $273,966, including 100 beds paid up front by the ALIVE
National Centre at the University of Melbourne in August. 40 beds were pressed at our own facility
and assembled in Maningrida with local workers.

**Where your money sits in the raise.** We are asking QBE for $300,000 to build two community
plants, and the Brian M. Davis Charitable Foundation for $100,000, of which $60,000 buys 80 beds.
Tim Fairfax has invited a three-year application for the organisation itself. Your $100,000 is bed
money, and it is the single largest bed line in the raise. Nothing is signed today.

**What you would get back.** A dated register extract naming every bed, the community it went to and
the date it arrived. Consented stories from the people who received them, where they have agreed to
be quoted. Both already exist for the beds delivered so far.
