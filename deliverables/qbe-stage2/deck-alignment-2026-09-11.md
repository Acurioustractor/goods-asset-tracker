## Seven findings, all fixed, worst first

### 1. Two slides print 130 beds as paid and delivered, six times between them

`ZZ4GO` S11 and `LX4ci` S12. Centrecorp paid for **167** beds across two invoices: 60 Basket Beds on
INV-0259 and 107 Stretch Beds on INV-0291. **130 is the quantity on quote QU-0014 from May 2026,
which nobody has paid.**

S11 carries it five times, including in the frame's own name:

- frame name "How 130 beds moved through relationships"
- the big number, "130 · Stretch Beds · paid and delivered"
- "Centrecorp bought 130 beds. The work moved through local hands."
- step 01 of the flow, "Centrecorp buys 130 Stretch Beds"
- the footer, "Paid and delivered: 130 beds."

S12 carries it once, as "PAID + DELIVERED · 130 beds · Centrecorp · Utopia".

**What is true.** Centrecorp bought 167 beds. 147 are deployed. 20 are made and waiting at Alice
Springs. At Utopia specifically there are 147 beds, of which 139 are Centrecorp's; the other 8 are
Stretch Beds from batch GB0-152, which Centrecorp did not pay for.

### 2. S11's reconciliation note is now out of date

`ZZ4GO` reads "Keep the 87, 130 and historic 147 records separate until overlap is reconciled." The
reconciliation was done on 11 September and ties exactly. The 87 is Utopia's Stretch Bed count, being
8 from GB0-152 and 79 from GB0-156.

### 3. S15 says ALIVE paid $75,000

`TiKvy` reads "ALIVE's 100 · already paid · $75,000". **ALIVE paid $101,200**, being $92,000 net for
100 Stretch Beds at $800 plus four half-share workshops at $3,000, settled 20 August 2026 on INV-0342.
The $75,000 was never on an invoice.

### 4. S17 says sixty beds a month

`rknzM` reads "then the 400 from December at sixty a month". Sixty is the ceiling at full
availability. **Ben set availability at 80% on 10 September, which is 48 a month**, and Q19 is
written on 48. The slide and the answer disagree.

### 5. S12 leaves out the two buyers that matter most

`LX4ci` shows only Centrecorp and Maningrida. It omits **ALIVE, 100 beds, the largest paid bed order
Goods has**, and Mala'la Health Service, 13 beds. It also carries no demand figures at all, while the
demand record holds 778 open beds across five communities.

### 6. S03's four statistics are not the four locked problem modules

`T814io` carries waste at 12.7% recycled in the NT, health at 20 times the RHD prevalence, employment
at 38% against 68% for 2022-23, and enterprise at 382 owner-managers. The four modules locked on
11 September carry different cuts: 87% of plastic to landfill, NT First Nations RHD at 3,398.7 per
100,000 with 506 new ARF diagnoses in 2024, employment at 35% against 62% on the 2021 Census, and
68.4% Indigenous employment across 3,327 Supply Nation businesses.

Both sets are real and sourced. The employment pair is the one to watch: 38% is the 2022-23 health
survey and 35% is the 2021 Census, so a reader who sees both without the survey named will assume an
error. Decide which instrument the deck uses and use it everywhere.

### 7. S18 rounds the module range

`tVcLc` reads "$96,000 to $143,000". The module total is **$95,767 to $142,467**. Both ends are rounded up, which makes the
$150,000 allowance look tighter than it is.
