## B · Why local production matters
At the website’s rounded numbers, a \$750 bed with a \$685 bought-kit marginal cost leaves about \$65 contribution; a \$426 locally pressed marginal cost leaves about \$324. The pressed cost is modelled, not a sustained-run actual. Contribution must still cover operating obligations. Keep product making, the shared organisation and community wraparound costs visible separately.
An optional future-state slide can show five plants × twenty beds per week × fifty weeks = 5,000 beds per year. The website’s \$1m illustration uses \$200 per bed. Treat both as targets; the \$200 requires an explicit cost definition before calling it net profit available to reinvest. Do not put this ambition before the current proof.
## C · Impact evidence framework
- Useful products: units built, allocated, delivered, in use, repaired and retired. Baseline from the current register; refresh before release. Suggested reporting: monthly operations and quarterly funder summary.
- Paid work: actual hours, roles and payments, separated by production, assembly and delivery. Modelled hours remain a forecast until timesheets exist.
- Materials: weighed input, finished material and offcuts per batch. Product design mass is not automatically measured waste diversion.
- Local control: signed rules and the six-month questions about keys, payroll, invoicing and local production. Record partial progress honestly.
- Lived experience: approved stories and correctly attributed quotations. Use them to understand experience, not to claim clinical outcomes.
Measurement ownership to assign: operations for units; finance for payments; production lead for run records; partner representatives for local decision evidence; authorised story steward for consent and attribution.
## D · Governance and capital evidence
The diagram must distinguish a legal entity, a business name, a partner, an adviser and a site operator. Goods on Country is the operating identity under The Butterfly Movement Ltd. ACT and historic sole-trader accounts explain the transition; they should not create a second current operating brand.
For each funding line record: funder; amount; instrument; applicant/recipient; current status; conditions; decision date; available date; source letter; callable contact; what QBE changes. Keep invited / asked / conditional / signed / received / paid purchase distinct.
Do not add a three-year grant in full to year-one cash. Do not count an alternative lender twice. Do not count ALIVE as both new capital and already-paid demand. Disclose related programmes separately until their scope and conditions are documented.
