# Community organisations as members of the charity (proposed)

> A working idea, not a decision (Ben, 16 September 2026). The community organisations that take Goods beds or run a production facility would become members of The Butterfly Movement Ltd, trading as Goods on Country. Nothing has changed in the constitution or the register of members, so every surface says "proposed" or "working on".

## The idea

Goods on Country is a company limited by guarantee, so it has members and no shareholders. The proposal is that the organisations closest to the beds become those members: the four community organisations that hold 100 beds each as stock, and any organisation that runs a community production facility.

## What membership would give them

- A vote at the charity's general meetings, including the annual general meeting.
- A say in how Goods on Country is run, from the organisations that hold the beds and run the facilities.
- No shares and no ownership of the charity: a company limited by guarantee has neither.

Membership does not change who controls a community organisation. Each one stays independent, with its own board, its own bed stock, its own sales money and its own decisions about who gets a bed.

## What has to happen before it is real

- The board agrees the idea.
- The full constitution is checked for how members are admitted, and whether a change is needed. The copy on the ACNC register ends at clause 17, so the full document is needed first.
- Each community organisation's own board agrees to become a member.
- The register of members is updated.

Until those are done, no page says an organisation is a member.
