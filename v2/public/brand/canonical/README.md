# Canonical Goods brand assets

The complete, current package now lives at [`/brand/goods/`](/brand/goods/).
It includes outlined SVG masters, transparent PNGs at three sizes, flattened
JPEGs, the identity guide and a downloadable ZIP.

The approved marks are:

- The complete `Goods.` wordmark in Archivo Bold 700.
- The grounded Goods on Country lockup, with sentence-case `on Country` and a
  terracotta ground rail.

The terracotta full stop is part of the mark. There is no standalone letter or
monogram. The former tracked, all-caps `ON COUNTRY` lockup is retired.

Source: `design/Goods_Final_Deck.pen` and
`tools/generate_goods_brand_kit.py`, approved 24 July 2026.
