# The raise

> Four applications are in flight and the first one closes at noon on Friday 25 September 2026. The year needs $747,950, $600,000 is asked across five lines, $0 is secured and the gap is $147,950. This page carries the whole raise in one place: what it is, the four forms with their dates, amounts and instruments, the money in four lines, and the one action worth more than any wording change, which is sending the SEFA loan EOI that is drafted and sitting unsent. The reading order at the bottom says which article to open for what, so a person or an agent arriving cold can do something useful inside two minutes.

## What the raise is

Goods on Country is raising one year of money: two community plants at the $150,000 allowance, 400 beds of first stock for four community pools, facilitation in those four communities, and the cost of running the organisation while community enterprises start trading. The Butterfly Movement Ltd is the applicant and the recipient on every grant, and Goods on Country is a registered business name of Butterfly (ruling AA). Zero community enterprises are trading yet, and that is the thing this year changes. Every bed sold does two jobs, because the $750 price covers making the bed and hands the balance to the organisation that gets the next one made, and both halves of that split are provisional until somebody cuts the bought leg panels and counts the yield.

Three things follow from that table and each one is worth knowing before a call.

**320 of the 400 beds have no sent ask against them**, which is $240,000 at the published $750. The unsent Snow ask is 133 of those, and sending it takes the gap to 187. Beds are the cheapest ask to fill, because bed money pays for its own making and hands the balance to the organisation, so a funder who buys beds also pays for part of the year.

**Plant money never moves the gap.** A plant costs $150,000 and a plant grant brings $150,000, so a plant either happens or it does not, and it leaves no hole behind it. The one exception is the second Commonwealth $150,000: if Ben names a site QBE is already being asked to fund, that money lands on a funded cost and closes the gap, and it then has to be declared at QBE Q14 and Q15.

**628 paid beds a year carries the whole organisation** and the year plans 400. That break-even is provisional, because it derives from the provisional $474 a bed contributes, and the bought leg panel yield is unknown until Nic cuts the 25 sheets already invoiced and counts what comes off them. The second press, about $22,500, is in no ask and nobody has quoted it.

## The one thing that would change the most

**Send the SEFA Backing the Bold EOI.** Jay's steer, recorded on the QBE final application page, is that QBE in 2026 wants corporate philanthropy to unlock debt or equity, and that a stack made entirely of philanthropy is something the Steerco would have to discuss. Our $600,000 is 100% philanthropy: QBE, Tim Fairfax, Brian M. Davis and Snow are all grants. The EOI is already written, the Queensland stream is open with rolling acceptance, and the form takes under fifteen minutes. A letter of intent does not have to be a signed deal, so one send changes the shape of the stack while the QBE application is still open.

One field decides it. SEFA asks what percentage of revenue comes from trade, with EBITDA for FY26, FY25, FY24 and FY23. Butterfly's FY26 EBITDA is about negative $42,854 against roughly $168,000 in the trading entity, so the field forces the entity question into the open. Joel Bird raised exactly this on 21 August and offered a call with Tanya from SEFA's investment team. That call happens first, and then the EOI goes.

## The trade record, in one line

Four organisations have bought and paid for 320 beds across five settled invoices: $273,966 including GST, $247,770 net. Name the basis every time. Facilitation is a second thing those same buyers already pay for, $50,000 net across ten visits at $3,000, $6,000 and $8,000, which is why the Brian M. Davis application asks for it as its own line. 540 beds are deployed across 11 communities (`asset-canonical.ts:33`), which counts what reached a household. That is a different count from the invoices and the two are never added.
