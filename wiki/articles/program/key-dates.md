## The collision on 25 September

Two applications are due on the same Friday and one person owns both. QBE closes at noon, which means the practical deadline for everything QBE needs is Thursday 24 September. Brian M. Davis has no stated hour and should be treated as the same day.

They are not the same kind of work. QBE wants 25 questions answered, seven upload fields filled and an accountant's letter that has no owner beside it on the application page. Brian M. Davis is drafted against their real form and splits by job, so the money is legible without a rewrite. What it is missing is one thing: the child safety question is in draft and must not be submitted until it is finished. Ben owns that.

The QBE submission blockers are held with what each one is worth in [[capital/the-blockers]], and the form itself is broken down question by question in [[qbe-stage-2-form]].

## 14 September, the last board meeting before both

Monday 14 September is eleven days before the collision and the only scheduled board meeting between now and it. Two QBE answers depend on what is minuted there.

Q8 is written and final, and one sentence in it says Kristy Bloomfield declares her interest at both boards and takes no part in a decision of either about a transaction between them. Submitting that sentence without the minute turns a disclosure into an exposure. Oonchiumpa should be asked to minute the same at its own board.

Q22 asks for governance documents. Butterfly's constitution has still not been located, and Eloise holds it. The minute from 14 September is the one governance document that can be added to that field on time.

## 18 September, the invoice and the shred on the same day

Defy invoice INV-2021 was issued on 11 September for 25 sheets of 19mm recycled panel cut to 800 by 1200, billed to A Curious Tractor, $10,496.20 including GST. It falls due on Friday 18 September.

Nic told Defy on 28 August that Witta burns about 450 kg of shred a week and had three weeks of stock left. Three weeks from 28 August is 18 September. The invoice and the material both land on the same Friday, and Defy's terms say work commences after receipt of payment.

Two quotes sit behind this and neither has been opened. QU0494 and QU0495 carry current shred pricing, and a bag price in them decides whether the second press is worth buying against bought panels. The second press is $22,500 and it is in no ask. Ben or Nic owns opening them, and there is no date on it, which is why it keeps not happening.

## The 13 November problem

QBE communicates conditional outcomes on 23 October and requires any preconditions to be met by 13 November. The Brian M. Davis board sits on 19 November. Tim Fairfax decides in late November.

So QBE's precondition date lands before either of the other two boards has decided. If the precondition is evidence of other funding, we cannot produce a commitment on 13 November that nobody will make until the 19th at the earliest. The question has been flagged since 2 September and is now asked plainly inside the Q18 answer: can a conditional outcome or a written invitation meet QBE's evidence requirement on 13 November. Jay Boolkin can answer it before 25 September, and if he does, the last paragraph of Q18 is replaced with his answer.

## SEFA has no date, which is the point

The Queensland stream of Backing the Bold is open with rolling acceptance and takes under fifteen minutes. The EOI is drafted and unsent. Nothing external is holding it.

The entity that was holding it is ruled: one entity, Butterfly, renamed Goods on Country, Ben 12 September. What holds it now is the call. Answered as Butterfly the required field reads an FY26 EBITDA of about negative $42,854 with almost no trade revenue, so the four-year history is the weak part of the case and a conversation is the right place to put it. Joel Bird flagged exactly this on 21 August and offered a call with Tanya from the investment team. That call should happen before the EOI goes. See [[governance/the-entity-question]] and [[investors/sefa]].

This is also the date that matters most to QBE. Jay Boolkin's steer for 2026 is that QBE wants corporate philanthropy to unlock debt or equity, and our $600,000 asked is 100% philanthropy. A sent EOI is the only thing in the calendar that changes that shape before 25 September.
