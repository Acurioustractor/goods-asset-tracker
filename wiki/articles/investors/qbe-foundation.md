# QBE Foundation

> Program anchor. Corporate foundation of QBE Insurance. Running Catalysing Impact 2026 in partnership with Social Impact Hub. Goods is a selected cohort enterprise.

## Mandate

Two priorities:
1. **Climate Resilience**: physical + financial risk understanding, mitigation options, low-carbon transition.
2. **Inclusion**: access to opportunity for excluded groups, thought leadership, financial inclusion skills.

Guiding principles: Sustainable Impact, Empowerment, Engagement, Strategic Alignment, Partner for Innovation.

## What they fund

- A catalytic grant, up to $400K per enterprise, awarded at QBE's discretion. The pool is up to $1.1M across ten enterprises, so $400K is the top of a range and not a plan. 2025 paid $1.02M across ten, averaging about $102K.
- The terms bind a coverage test on the grant: it must be at least matched by signed external commitments. That is not dollar for dollar, there is no floor, and a signature obliges QBE to nothing. See [[../capital/what-we-no-longer-say]].
- Skilled volunteering time from QBE employees.
- Hackathon days (1-day per enterprise, August).
