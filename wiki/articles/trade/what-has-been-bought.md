# What has been bought

> Four organisations have paid for 320 beds across five settled invoices: $273,966 including GST, $247,770 net. Centrecorp Foundation paid for 167, ALIVE at the University of Melbourne paid for 100 before a single bed was made, Homeland School Company paid for 40 and Mala'la Health Service paid for 13. The same buyers paid $50,000 net for ten community visits alongside the beds, which is the evidence that facilitation is something buyers already price. Invoices are not the asset register: 320 beds invoiced and 540 beds deployed count two different things and are never added.

## The two totals, and the basis

Every line here is read off an invoice in one Xero organisation, the sole trader Goods trades through today (Nicholas Marchesi, ABN 21 591 780 066, read 11 September 2026). Including GST the five invoices settled for **$273,966**, which is what reached the bank. Net of GST they come to **$247,770**, which is what a profit and loss sees.

Name the basis in the same sentence as the number. The two figures describe the same five invoices, and a funder checking either one against the accounts will be using one or the other.

These are invoices raised and settled in one organisation. They are not consolidated accounts and they are not the asset register.

## The five invoices

### INV-0259 · Centrecorp Foundation · 60 beds to Utopia Homelands

Raised 11 August 2025, paid in full 4 September 2025. 60 Basket Beds at $370 each, $22,200 net, plus two bed-building workshops at $6,000 each. Total $34,200 net, $37,620 including GST. Invoiced as "Goods Basket Bed v1.3". The Basket Bed is the first prototype, sales are discontinued and the design is being open-sourced, so this price says nothing about what Goods sells today. See [[products/basket-bed-legacy]].

### INV-0283 · Mala'la Health Service Aboriginal Corporation · 13 beds to Maningrida

Raised 21 October 2025, paid in full 21 November 2025. 13 Basket Beds at $380 each, $4,940 net, $5,434 including GST. Shipping was quoted at $3,200 on its own line and then discounted in full, so Goods carried freight worth 65% of the bed line on a $4,940 order. This is the invoice behind the rule that freight is now its own line at cost. See [[trade/price-and-freight]].

### INV-0291 · Centrecorp Foundation · 107 beds to Utopia Homelands

Raised 26 November 2025, paid in full 3 February 2026. Sixty-nine days to settle, the longest of the five. 107 Stretch Beds at $560 each, $59,920 net, plus three bed-building workshops at $6,000 each. Total $77,920 net, $85,712 including GST. The invoice reads "Goods Weave Bed v2.3 - Utopia Homelands" and those beds are Stretch Beds; the naming is ruled and is covered below.

### INV-0303 · Homeland School Company · 40 beds to the Maningrida homelands

Raised 18 May 2026, paid in full 23 July 2026. 40 Stretch Beds at $750 each, the first invoice at list price, $30,000 net. One Goods on Country program-support trip at $8,000 to train, build, test, document and make with community, travel and accommodation included. Two Indestructible Washing Machines v1.1 at $4,500 each, $9,000. Freight $5,900 as its own line. A Goods in Kind Partnership line took $14,190 off the face of the invoice, 27% of it before the deduction was applied. Total $38,710 net, $44,000 including GST.

This is the only invoice carrying beds, a washer, facilitation and freight together. These are the 40 beds pressed at Witta and assembled at Gamardi with young people. See [[communities/maningrida]].

### INV-0342 · ALIVE National Centre, University of Melbourne · 100 beds

Raised 2 July 2026, paid in full 20 August 2026. 100 Stretch Beds at $800 each, $80,000 net, plus four half-shares of a bed-building workshop at $3,000 each, the buyer carrying half the cost of each visit. Total $92,000 net, **$101,200 including GST**. ALIVE paid up front, before a bed was made.

The largest bed order settled to date and the only one above the $750 list price. The $75,000 that used to appear on the deck was never on an invoice and is retired; the correct figure is $101,200 including GST, $92,000 net. ALIVE is a paid institutional buyer as well as a research relationship. See [[capital/what-we-no-longer-say]].

## Centrecorp: 167 paid, 147 deployed, 20 waiting

Centrecorp bought and paid for 167 beds across INV-0259 and INV-0291, and every one of them has a register row. Batch GB0-148 holds the 60 Basket Beds, all deployed at Utopia. Batch GB0-156 holds exactly 107 Stretch Beds, split three ways: 79 deployed at Utopia, 8 deployed at Alice Springs, and 20 made and ready at Alice Springs. Those 20 are the whole of the gap between 167 and 147.

Two traps sit in that paragraph.

- Utopia's community total is also 147, and it is a different 147. Utopia holds 60 Basket and 79 Stretch from the Centrecorp batches plus 8 Stretch from batch GB0-152, which Centrecorp did not pay for. The two figures share a number and do not share a single bed. Never treat one as evidence for the other.
- Quote QU-0014 for 130 beds, May 2026, was never paid. Nothing in the register or the ledger is 130. Batch GB0-152 is 15 units, 8 at Utopia and 7 at Tennant Creek, and no invoice here accounts for it. That is a separate thread.

Centrecorp's beds are delivered. They are never counted as demand (Ben, 11 September 2026). See [[trade/the-four-doors]].

## Invoices are not the register

The register counts 540 beds deployed across eleven communities, and that count includes prototypes and gifted beds. The invoice record counts 320 beds bought and paid for. The two count different things and are never added together, in either direction.

A paid invoice records what a buyer did. It says nothing about what that buyer will do next, so no line on this page is a forward order or a pipeline.

## Money owed by a buyer on this list

INV-0341, ALIVE National Centre, $66,000 including GST: a twelve-month Empathy Ledger storytelling programme at $5,000 a month, with no beds on the invoice. Authorised and unpaid as at 11 September 2026, past its 30 July 2026 due date.

It was raised the same day as INV-0342, which ALIVE has paid in full, so a receivables list reads worse than the bed trade does. Carry both invoices together whenever either one is shown.

## The one document that must never be corrected

INV-0291 and quote QU-0014 both read "Goods Weave Bed v2.3". Those are Stretch Beds. The line name was held steady across the Centrecorp paper trail on purpose so their finance team sees the same description invoice to invoice (Ben, 2 August 2026). Do not correct either document, and do not read them as evidence that a Weave Bed was ever produced at scale. Randle Walker calls them Stretch beds in correspondence, which is the right name everywhere else. See [[products/stretch-bed]].
