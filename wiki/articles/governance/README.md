# Governance

> Governance is a live build. Goods has founder leadership, a Goods-specific advisory group, a newly formalising structure and a strong story-sovereignty intent. It does not yet have a finished board, legal vehicle or investor-ready governance pack. Two funders now read this folder before they read the model: the Tim Fairfax form tests governance maturity, and Butterfly's unlocated constitution blocks two QBE questions and one Tim Fairfax field at once. The open half of the entity question is which entity holds the revenue from the 320 beds buyers have paid for.
