# Community-Controlled Capability Pilot Plan

**Date:** 24 July 2026  
**Status:** Draft for approval before implementation  
**Pilot sequence:** Tennant Creek → Utopia / Urapuntja → Oonchiumpa / Mparntwe → Palm Island / Bwgcolman

## The outcome

Build one repeatable Goods process that helps a community:

1. name what it wants to make possible;
2. document the people, strengths and infrastructure already present;
3. select only the Goods modules it wants;
4. identify the support required to operate and own them;
5. create a complete project budget;
6. connect the project directly to suitable resources;
7. deliver, train, measure and return the evidence; and
8. decide what happens next.

Goods supplies the shared technical backbone. The community controls the purpose, assets, operating decisions, evidence and next step.

## System roles

| System | Role | Must not become |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Goods community system | Operational source of truth for places, partners, requests, modules, readiness, costs, assets, delivery and next decisions | A duplicate story archive |
| Empathy Ledger | People, interviews, photos, video, consent, permitted audiences and community review | A project-management database |
| Notion | Draft plans, meeting notes, supplier quotes, applications, actions and decision preparation | The canonical community database |
| Miro | Visual opportunity map, case-study boards, funder workshops and slide development | The only record of a decision |

Every record should reference stable Goods IDs for the place, partner organisation, program and pathway.

## The shared end-to-end process

### 0. Prepare

- Confirm the invitation, lead relationship and community-controlled organisation.
- Separate the place, organisation, program and Goods pathway.
- Gather existing Goods records, Empathy Ledger material and Notion documents.
- Prepare a one-page evidence pack showing what is verified, assumed and unknown.

**Output:** pre-conversation evidence sheet.

### 1. Permission and authority

- Confirm who holds cultural, organisational and operating authority.
- Agree who participates and how decisions are made.
- Agree what may be recorded and what may be shared.
- Confirm who can approve the final pathway.

**Output:** conversation and decision protocol.

### 2. Community aspiration

- Ask what the community wants to make possible.
- Ask what is already working.
- Ask why this matters now.
- Identify the smallest useful first step.
- Record the aspiration in the community's own words.

**Output:** approved aspiration statement.

### 3. Capability map

- People and skills
- Governance and organisations
- Site, power and access
- Existing equipment
- Materials and waste streams
- Customers and demand
- Delivery and logistics
- Training, safety and maintenance
- Existing programs, funding and partners

**Output:** Community Capability Map.

### 4. Select modules

The community chooses from an open menu:

- product access and delivery
- collection and sorting
- shredding
- pressing and material production
- CNC and fabrication
- assembly and repair
- workforce and enterprise
- demand and contracts
- finance and working capital
- story and evidence
- maintenance and technical support

**Output:** Community Module Configuration.

### 5. Readiness and support

For each selected module, agree:

- owner
- operator
- site
- safety requirements
- training
- feedstock
- demand or offtake
- maintenance and spares
- working capital
- Goods support requested
- stop, pause and review points

**Output:** Readiness and Support Map.

### 6. Complete-project costing

Cost:

- equipment
- freight, access and site preparation
- local wages and participation
- training and commissioning
- first production run
- safety and quality systems
- maintenance, consumables and spares
- demand and enterprise development
- working capital
- evidence and community reporting
- contingency

Label every figure verified, estimated, modelled or unknown.

**Output:** Community-Controlled Investment Case.

### 7. Ownership and implementation agreement

- Community entity owns or has a documented pathway to own the assets.
- Local governance controls operating decisions and revenue.
- Goods' technical role and optional ongoing support are explicit.
- Data, stories and public communication remain community-controlled.
- The community can change providers and determine what happens next.

**Output:** Ownership, Support and Decision Agreement.

### 8. Deliver, learn and return

- Procure and deliver.
- Train local operators.
- Complete a measured first run.
- Record costs, time, quality, participation and maintenance.
- Return the evidence to the community first.
- Publish only the community-approved case study.

**Output:** operating record, community report and approved case study.

### 9. Community chooses the next step

- continue
- add another module
- change the configuration
- pause
- transfer more responsibility
- share learning with another community

**Output:** next community-controlled decision.
