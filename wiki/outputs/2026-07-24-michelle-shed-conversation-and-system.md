**Purpose:** Reopen the Tennant Creek recycling-facility pathway with Michelle, connect the Shed and Youth Centre opportunity, and establish one clean operating and storytelling workflow.

## Phone conversation guide

### Opening

“I do not want to bring you another finished plan. I want to pick up what we have already done, hear what the Shed wants now, and work out the smallest useful next step together.”

### 1. Start with Michelle and the Shed

- How are things going at the Shed?
- What is most important for the Shed over the next 12 months?
- Is local plastic collection and production still something the Shed wants to lead?
- What parts of the earlier proposal still feel right? What no longer fits?

### 2. Reflect the existing work

“We currently have the proposal as six months of collection, shredding, training and baseline data, followed by six months adding pressing and CNC production. The earlier price was $234,000 excluding GST, before power and local wages. Is that still the pathway you want us to develop?”

Confirm:

- whether the staged structure remains useful;
- whether rental, purchase or a pathway to ownership is preferred;
- site, power, access, storage, insurance and safety;
- likely operators and supervision;
- local wages and working capital; and
- who must approve the final structure.

### 3. Understand the CBF pathway

- What exactly did CBF say was missing or non-compliant?
- What evidence does the Shed now have?
- Who should lead the application and who should support it?
- Does the scope or budget need to change?
- What must be ready before the current round closes?

Do not promise that the existing quote is still current until equipment, freight and support costs are reconfirmed.

### 4. Define the Youth Centre relationship

- What role does Michelle see for the Youth Centre?
- What do the young people and Youth Centre manager want?
- Could participation begin with builds, repair, collection or design before operating machinery?
- Who holds youth safety, supervision and program responsibility?
- What would a useful first activity look like?

Keep the Shed, Youth Centre and Goods as separate organisations with explicit responsibilities.

### 5. Discuss story as community infrastructure

Suggested framing:

“We already have images and video of young people building a bed. I do not want those to become content that leaves Tennant Creek without giving something useful back. What would the Shed and young people want to keep, learn from or share?”

Ask:

- Who owns and approves the overall Shed story?
- Who needs to approve use of youth images and voices?
- Which material is public, funder-only or internal?
- Would people value prints, a short film, a private gallery, a community report or skills to make their own stories?
- What should funders understand that a normal acquittal would miss?
- Who should review and withdraw material if circumstances change?

Explain Empathy Ledger simply:

“Empathy Ledger is the place where people, stories, media, consent and allowed audiences are held. It does not replace the Shed's authority. It helps us honour it.”

### 6. Close with an approval loop

Read back:

- what Michelle said the Shed wants;
- what is confirmed;
- what is still unknown;
- each person's next action and date; and
- what Goods will send back for approval.

End with:

“We will not present this as the Tennant Creek model until you have checked that we heard it correctly.”

## One operating system, with clear roles

Use GoHighLevel, but use it as the relationship and follow-up layer only. Do not turn it into a second asset register, story archive or document library.

| Information | Canonical home | What links to it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contact, organisation, emails, calls, next action, relationship stage | **GoHighLevel** | Goods place, partner and pathway IDs |
| Community request, selected modules, readiness, costs, assets, delivery, decisions | **Goods community system** | GHL opportunity, Notion workspace, Empathy Ledger project or collection |
| People, interviews, photos, video, consent, permitted audiences, review | **Empathy Ledger** | Goods community and pathway IDs |
| Meeting notes, draft application, supplier quotes, working task list | **Notion** | Canonical records above |
| Public pages, approved case studies, product information | **Goods website/codebase** | Approved Goods and Empathy Ledger records |
| Opportunity map, workshop and funder presentation | **Miro** | Links to canonical records, never the only copy |

### GoHighLevel setup

Create:

- **Organisation:** Our Community Shed Inc.
- **Primary contact:** Michelle Bates.
- **Opportunity:** Tennant Creek Community Production Pathway.
- **Pipeline stages:** Invited → Listening → Community brief approved → Modules selected → Ready to cost → Funding pathway → Agreement → Delivery → Operating → Community review.

Minimum opportunity fields:

- Goods place ID
- Goods partner ID
- Goods pathway ID
- community-stated aspiration
- current stage
- lead authority
- next action
- next-action owner
- next-action date
- CBF status and deadline
- Notion workspace URL
- Empathy Ledger collection or project URL
- last community approval date

Do not store youth consent detail, sensitive stories or raw media in GoHighLevel. Store only the appropriate record link and a safe status such as `story review pending`.

## What to send partners

There should not be one giant artifact. Use a small linked set.

### Before the first conversation

Send only the email and, if useful, a one-page conversation sheet. Do not send a large funder deck before listening.

### After the conversation

Send a **two-page PDF community brief**:

1. “What we heard”: aspiration, existing strengths, requested modules and principles.
2. “What happens next”: unknowns, actions, owners, dates and approval request.

A PDF is best here because it is stable, easy to email, print, forward and approve.

### During development

Use a **private shared Notion page** as the living workspace for meeting notes, current actions, draft budget and documents. Use it only if it is genuinely easy for Michelle and the Shed. Export important approval points to PDF so they do not depend on Notion access.

### For story and evidence

Use an **Empathy Ledger collection or private evidence gallery** for approved photos, video, voices, consent and audience settings. Send a direct, simple link rather than expecting the partner to navigate the full platform.

### Once jointly approved

Publish a **Goods website case-study page**. This is the durable, funder-facing story and can combine:

- the community-approved aspiration;
- the selected module pathway;
- verified delivery and operating data;
- approved photos, video and voices from Empathy Ledger;
- the investment required;
- current status; and
- the next community-controlled decision.

The website page is the public source. The PDF is the portable snapshot. Notion is the working room. Empathy Ledger is the consent-bearing story record.

## Boundaries and current unknowns

- The Dusseldorp and ALIVE conversations provide useful principles and relationships. They do not constitute Shed approval.
- The earlier plant price must be revalidated.

- Existing media should not be treated as automatically approved for every new audience or purpose. Audience and use should be recorded in Empathy Ledger.
