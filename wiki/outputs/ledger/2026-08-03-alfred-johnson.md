# Alfred, Palm Island

![Alfred Johnson, Palm Island](../../../v2/public/images/people/alfred-johnson.jpg)

"You can't just go down to the store and buy beds and stuff, it's hard for families and, especially over here, you won't just have a family, one family in one house. You'll have multiple families, in the one house and not everyone gets a bed, and not everyone has that place to stay."

Alfred is explaining what the barge does to a bed. Everything on Palm Island comes over on it, and the freight lands on families before the furniture does. He was one of the first people to sit down with Goods and lay the economics out plainly, house by house.

Alfred Johnson, Palm Island, June 2026.
