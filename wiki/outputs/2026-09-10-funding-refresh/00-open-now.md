## The eligibility gate moved and nothing has caught up

The funding-pipeline skill rules out a class of First Nations finance on the basis that Goods is
"a for-profit Pty Ltd, not 50%+ First Nations owned yet". Ruling AA on 5 September made
The Butterfly Movement Ltd the applicant and recipient. Butterfly is a charity, so:

- **FRRR SRC is open to us and was not.** Its wording excludes "an individual, sole trader,
  private business or commercial business" and requires a not-for-profit with an ABN. A Pty Ltd
  cannot apply. Butterfly can.
- **IBA turns on a word nobody has checked.** Its threshold is 50% Indigenous "ownership **or
  membership**". A company limited by guarantee has members, not shareholders, so the test for
  Butterfly is its membership, which is defined in the constitution. That is the same document
  Q12 and Q22 are waiting on. The constitution is not only an application attachment; it decides
  whether First Nations finance is available at all.

Neither of these is a decision I can make. Both need a person who can read the constitution and,
for IBA, probably a call to IBA.
