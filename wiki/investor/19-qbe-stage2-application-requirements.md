## What the application requires

Jay's list, verbatim in substance. Each line is a real deliverable, and several are open.

**Corporate structure**
- Full list of related entities with ABNs/ACNs
- An ownership/structure diagram if relevant
- A list of directors across all related entities

⚠ This lands directly on the open entity question. A Curious Tractor Pty Ltd
(ABN 36 697 347 676), The Butterfly Movement Ltd (ABN 22 155 132 684), A Kind Tractor (dormant),
and the fact that Goods still trades through a sole trader during migration, all have to be drawn
honestly on one page.

**Funding request**
- Amount, use of funds, and expected impact

**Commitments from funders**
- Amount, source, type, **and contact details so we can verify these**

⚠ **This is ruling M confirmed by the program itself.** What counts as match paper is a letter
naming amount, instrument, funder legal name and a contact SIH can call. Not a facility
agreement. The "at least three signed LOIs" gate that was struck for having no source is
confirmed absent here too: Jay states no count. It is a dollar test with verifiable paper.

**Readiness to use funds**
- How quickly the grant could be deployed

**Supporting materials**
- **Pitch materials: "simply whatever you've already used to secure external capital, so no need
  to create anything new."** Worth reading twice before building anything for this.
- Evidence of funder commitments
- Track record of previous payments, for example loans or debt paid off
- Evidence of impact, for example an impact measurement report or theory of change
- **Full financials: P&L, Balance Sheet, Cashflow**
- Governance documents: Constitution, Shareholders Agreement and similar

**Declarations**
- Confirming the information is accurate
- **Proof of audit, or an accountant's letter**
- Disclosure of any legal, compliance or regulatory matters

⚠ **The accountant's letter is now a hard requirement, not an open action.** The Goods-only
FY26 revenue carve-out of $713,827 is a **workpaper** and is explicitly NOT accountant-signed.
`STRATEGY.md` section 8 has carried "who signs the accountant letter, what artifact" as an open
item with no date. This email turns it into a submission blocker.

## What this changes about how we work

1. **The ask cannot name a submission date.** It can name that the program closes late September
   and that our own paper gate is earlier. Those are two different things and the copy must not
   collapse them.
2. **The internal gate is ours and should be described as ours.** A funder letter arriving on the
   day the form closes is useless: SIH has to ring the contact and verify it first. The buffer is
   the point, not an arbitrary deadline.
3. **The pitch page is enough.** "No need to create anything new" means `/pitch/road` and the
   existing material satisfy the pitch-materials line. Effort belongs on the financials, the
   accountant's letter, the entity diagram and actual signed commitments.
4. **3 September is the last group check-in.** It is the cheapest remaining chance to ask Jay the
   open questions in person, and it falls before the close.
