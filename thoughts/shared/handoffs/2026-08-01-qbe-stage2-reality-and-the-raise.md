## 1. The headline: $400K was never the plan

**A Curious Tractor is one of TEN** enterprises in the 2026 QBE Catalysing Impact cohort
(announced 23 March 2026). The others: Adapt Homes, Envirotech Education, Good Cycles, Hepburn
Energy, Mycelia Renewables, Plate It Forward, Revolve Recycling, RoboFit, Ngardara Project.

| | |
|---|---|
| 2026 pool | **up to AU$1.1 million, across all ten** |
| 2025 actual | AU$1.02M across ten, averaging **~AU$102,000** |
| SIH's stated range | "typically AU$150,000 to AU$400,000" |
| 2025 leverage | $1.02M grants leveraged $2.75M external, **3.7x** |

**So AU$400,000 is the top of the range and 36% of the whole pool.** Every Goods surface had it
as the plan.

**The mechanic runs the other way from how we described it.** SIH calls the money
**"catalytic, helping you unlock additional investment"**, never matching. The recorded terms
bind the **grant**: it "must be at least matched by SIGNED external commitments". That is a
coverage test on whatever QBE decides to give. **Raising $400,000 creates no obligation on QBE.**
There is **no $150K floor** either; that was a typical range hardened into a guarantee.

Full research, committed: `wiki/investor/20-qbe-program-economics.md`.
Jay's requirement list, committed: `wiki/investor/19-qbe-stage2-application-requirements.md`.

**What follows.** If QBE lands at $100-200K against a bottom-up need of $367-620K, the repayable
column is the raise, not the balance, and SEFA is the whole game. And leverage is the metric they
publish, so a large signed external stack against a modest ask argues their own scorecard back
at them.

**Three that stop an application.**

**COGS is zero.** Goods makes and sells a physical product; materials sit in an operating
expense line. There is **no gross margin anywhere in the accounts**, so the entire per-bed story
($750 sells, $685 costs, $65 stays, $324 pressed) has no counterpart in the books.

**83.6% of income is in a bucket with no name.** The $713,827 carve-out is extracted from a line
that is not itself classified. That is where any accountant asked to sign will start, and it is
probably why none has.

**Negative half a million of working capital**, sitting alongside farm equipment, veterinary,
rental property and eco-tourism accounts. It is Nic's whole financial life, not Goods.

**And $967,817 of non-current liabilities against $0.84 of interest** needs explaining before
anyone claims the debt-servicing record Jay asks for.

**The entity problem.** The cohort entrant is **A Curious Tractor** (incorporated; SIH requires
Australian-incorporated applicants). All trading and all financial statements sit in a sole
trader that is not the applicant. `STRATEGY.md` records an "entity mismatch in the QBE agreement"
never raised with SIH. That is now the gap between the applicant and its own accounts.
