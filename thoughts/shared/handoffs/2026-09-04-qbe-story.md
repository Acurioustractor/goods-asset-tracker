## What it is

Two routes over one component (`v2/src/app/sites/qbe/story/qbe-story.tsx`), told by Goods on
Country in the first person:

- **`/pitch/model`, open.** Fifteen chapters, eight drawings, twenty-two questions. No form
  questions, no deck frames, no named foundation or lender, no open decision, no calendar. Listed
  as an appendix of `/pitch/road` in `pitch-chrome.ts`. The pitch layout keeps it noindexed and
  reachable by link; indexing it is a separate call.
- **`/sites/qbe/story`, behind the investors gate.** Seventeen chapters, nine drawings, every
  funder line by name, who asked each question, the deck map and what is still open.

The `audience` switch (`'public' | 'working'`) is the only difference. `cruxFor`, `chaptersFor`,
`faqFor`, `diagramsFor` and each drawing take it. Guards assert the public surface never carries
a foundation or lender name, a first name, a file name, a form question, "subject to", "fallback"
or "has not ruled".

The working copy's seventeen chapters in five parts:
the story (crux, problem, road), the model (one bed, the loop, who buys), the money (three jobs,
catalytic, the snowball), the proof (who decides, the entity, the measured run, what changes, the
calendar), the questions (what is honest, the FAQ, to the deck). Contents bar with scrollspy,
`?view=slides` for presenting, print to one chapter per page (the Q9 PDF).
