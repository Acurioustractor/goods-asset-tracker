# Note to the other ACT project leads: two custom objects

**Draft for Ben to send. Not sent.** Audience: whoever runs Harvest, JusticeHub and CONTAINED in the
shared GHL sub-account (`agzsSZWgovjwgpcoASWG`).

---

Goods wants to claim two of the sub-account's ten custom object slots: **Community** and
**Organisation**.

The reason is that we currently model both as contacts, and it has produced 712 tags in the shared
account, about 120 of them `goods-*`. Twenty of those are `goods-community-<place>` tags that
duplicate `place:community:<place>` for the same place. A community is not a person and neither is a
land council, and every month we model them as people the tag list grows and the account gets harder
for all four projects to read.

What the two objects would hold:

- **Community.** The place, who speaks for it, the named Goods person who holds the relationship,
  what we promised and whether we did it, and a pause switch for sorry business. Associated to the
  people in that place, who stay as contacts.
- **Organisation.** Buyers, health services, shires and land councils, with their ABN, their
  procurement route, and the people who work there.

What it would not do: touch any tag, contact, pipeline or workflow belonging to Harvest, JusticeHub
or CONTAINED. Custom objects are additive, and the other eight slots stay free.

If Community or Organisation would be more useful as shared objects across all four projects rather
than Goods-owned ones, that is a better outcome and we would rather build it that way. Say so and we
will design them jointly.

If nobody objects by [DATE], we will create the two and report back what they contain.

---

**Context for Ben, not for sending:** the limit is 10 custom objects per sub-account across all
plans, confirmed in HighLevel's own documentation on 17 September 2026. Associations support
one-to-one through many-to-many with up to 10 relationship labels between any two object types, so
"this person speaks for this community" and "this org buys for these places" are both expressible.
