# QBE people funding alignment

**Date:** 2026-08-24  
**Question:** How should the people Goods is asking QBE to fund align with the work, entity, capital source, evidence and next decision?

## Bottom line

Do not present QBE with a generic payroll request or a list of people whose availability Goods wants to preserve. Present a small set of **time-bounded functions that remove named constraints and produce the next proof**. Each funded role should have one primary cost centre, one organisational job, one money door, one accountable legal home and one release/continuation gate.

The clean catalytic proposition is:

> QBE buys the finite people-time needed to turn founder-carried work into a community-authorised, measurable operating system: prove production economics, convert real buyer demand, establish responsibility for operators/assets/warranty, and make the community-production transfer pathway governable. Procurement and order-backed finance then take over the repeatable commercial work.

This fits GrantScope's thesis that catalytic capital de-risks transition points—measured production, cost-down, governance, productive capacity and the evidence needed to crowd in later capital—while procurement pays for repeat production and delivery.[^1]

## Why these people, not generic headcount

The current diagnostic says Ben and Nicholas are the two effective FTEs, paid through ad hoc contractor-style arrangements, and identifies the next constraints as sales, operations, procurement, production management, finance/modeling, governance and growing community-relationship capacity. It explicitly recommends hires that remove constraints rather than make ACT look larger.[^8]

That supports a QBE request built around four linked work packages rather than named-person retention:

1. **Prove production** — production/operations leadership plus measurement support.
2. **Prove demand and contribution** — institutional buyer/sales capacity.
3. **Prove responsibility and transfer** — community coordination, governance and local operator pathway.
4. **Make the proof investable** — fractional finance/commercial governance and evidence infrastructure.

Existing founders can be costed where they perform these functions, but the application should name the **role, allocation, outputs and replacement/handover path**, not imply that “funding Ben and Nic” is itself catalytic. The transition claim is that founders codify and transfer a system others can operate.

## Budget architecture

Use separate personnel lines under the three cost centres:

- **Product making:** direct labour for the measured run and later ordinary production. QBE may fund the one bounded proof; product revenue must carry repeat production.
- **Goods network:** production-system leadership, quality/training, institutional buyer work, procurement, back office and finance capability. This is shared across places and must not be copied into every community-site budget.
- **Community wraparound:** community coordination, participation, employment brokerage, governance, learning and local support. This is public-good work by design and must not be hidden in the bed price.[^9]

For every personnel line show: function; FTE or days; rate; dates; cost centre; employing/contracting entity; named deliverable; evidence produced; decision enabled; what pays after QBE. Where a person spans two centres, split their time explicitly rather than blending the budget.

## Catalytic sequence and credible leverage

QBE is one pool of up to $1.1M across ten enterprises; $400K is the top of a range, not an entitlement. The grant is described as catalytic and must be covered by signed external commitments; raising $400K does not oblige QBE to award $400K.[^10] Therefore the application should give a **modular ask**, with a core proof package and clear additions, rather than anchoring the whole workforce plan to the maximum award.

The leverage story should be causal:

1. QBE funds bounded people-time and transition costs.
2. That work produces community authority, measured costs, buyer paper, clarified responsibility and documented entity/fund flows.
3. Those proofs unlock the appropriate next money: procurement for products, order-backed working capital, asset finance/patient investment, and complementary philanthropy for wraparound.
4. Each later dollar has a release condition; a prospect or application is not leverage until evidenced.

The current legal-home split remains useful but needs resolving before submission: the working model sends repayable equipment/working capital to A Curious Tractor and grant-funded measured-run/running-cover/site-scoping work to The Butterfly Movement. It also warns that charity grant funds supporting trading-entity running costs require a defensible inter-entity agreement.[^11]

## Application language to use

> QBE funding will buy the finite capability required to move Goods from founder-carried delivery to a repeatable, community-authorised operating system. We will fund production and operations leadership for a measured run; buyer capacity to convert validated interest into authorised orders; community coordination and governance to define local roles and responsibility; and fractional finance/evidence support to make the resulting economics and fund flows investable. The grant-funded work ends at defined proof gates. Product orders then pay for ordinary making and delivery; order-backed finance supports working capital only after buyer commitment; and catalytic or patient capital supports productive assets only after ownership, operation, maintenance and transfer responsibilities are agreed.

## Non-negotiable evidence and decision gates

- **Opportunity evidence:** official source, named round, current close/rolling status, applicant eligibility, amount, concrete project fit, source URL and retrieval timestamp. Passing this gate only makes the opportunity eligible for human review.[^12]
- **Demand:** signed, authorised or contracted demand—not modelled need, facility interest or a CRM stage.
- **Production:** measured labour, yield, energy, quality and throughput—not a spreadsheet cost alone.
- **Responsibility:** named employer/operator and named warranty, maintenance, depreciation and insurance responsibility.
- **Transfer:** named asset owner, seller, margin holder and decision rights at each milestone.
- **Community authority:** explicit authority and an approved account of what was asked, done and learned.
- **Financial structure:** applicant, recipient, employing entity and fund flows reconcile; grant, trade and repayable capital are not blended.
