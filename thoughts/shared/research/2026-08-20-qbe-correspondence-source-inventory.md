## 1. The letter request: the non-negotiables

The current authority is [QBE Stage 2 application requirements](../../../wiki/investor/19-qbe-stage2-application-requirements.md).  It records Jay Boolkin's 14 and 20 July emails, and says that the application needs commitments with **amount, source, type and callable contact details**.  The source describes the evidence standard as a letter that names the **amount, instrument, funder legal name and contact SIH can call**.

Use this request in every commitment-letter conversation:

1. State the legal funder name and named signatory/contact.
2. State the amount and currency.
3. State the instrument: grant, loan, recoverable grant, equipment finance, etc.
4. State the intended receiving entity, or explicitly say `TBC` pending the entity decision.
5. State any conditions, decision authority and expected timing/disbursement.
6. Give permission for SIH to verify with the named contact, including their email/phone.

Do **not** call the request a guaranteed `$400,000` dollar-for-dollar match. QBE is a discretionary shared pool (up to `$1.1m` across ten enterprises); the grant actually awarded must be covered by signed external commitments. [`2026-08-01 QBE reality handoff`](../handoffs/2026-08-01-qbe-stage2-reality-and-the-raise.md), [`QBE program economics`](../../../wiki/investor/20-qbe-program-economics.md), and [the current QBE alignment review](2026-08-20-qbe-funding-pipeline-alignment-review.md) agree on this.

## 4. Recommended correspondence sequence

1. **Recipient-specific email:** one sentence on the existing relationship, one explicit ask for a documented indication/commitment, and the six letter fields above. Offer Jay/SIH as verifier.
2. **Attach/link only the SIH funder letter and live pitch.** Add the one-pager only if the recipient asks for a concise PDF.
3. **After interest:** send the right tailored brief (SEFA or Snow) and a clean draft-letter template; do not bulk-send sensitive finance/entity material.
4. **On receipt:** record the source in GHL and the Notion QBE commitment register; verify legal name, amount, instrument, entity, conditions and callable contact; ask SIH to accept it before it counts.
