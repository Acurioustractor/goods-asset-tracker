# Goods web story surface audit

Reviewed 29 July 2026 before building `/pitch/road`.

## Surface review

| Surface | Health | What it contributes | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/pitch` | Needs reframing | Strong visual polish and useful existing proof links | Move from a model-first sales page toward the road, relationships, authority and decision sequence |
| `/story/road` | Strong | Best long-form road narrative and cleared community voices | Reuse its human sequence and voice discipline |
| `/story` | Partial | Existing editorial entry point | Keep as a wider story index rather than making it carry the investment case |
| `/communities` | Strong | Place index and live geographic context | Bring a focused, interactive version into the pitch and preserve deep links |
| `/pathways` | Strong | Clear authority, consent and decision logic | Use as the detailed live proof behind the pitch summary |
| `/pitch/deck` | Canonical deck | Current pitch content, evidence labels and financial framing | Treat as the content source; translate it into a web-native scroll rather than duplicating a slide viewer |

## Synthesis

The strongest web story is:

1. Begin with the road and the people.
2. Show the seven stops as the residue of relationships, not a rollout funnel.
3. Let place be explored through a live map and direct community links.
4. Explain the six-stage community-making model only after the road is understood.
5. Keep evidence status, unknowns and unresolved definitions visible.
6. End with the next responsible decision and routes into the live proof system.

## Build outcome

The resulting route is `/pitch/road`. It uses the canonical deck data, the existing community location model, cleared storyteller records, real Goods photography and live links into the broader Goods site.
